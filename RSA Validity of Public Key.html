What this tool checks

  • PEM format and ability to import as SPKI (SubjectPublicKeyInfo).
  • Fallback parsing for PKCS#1 "RSA PUBLIC KEY" DER structure.
  • Extract modulus (n) & exponent (e) and compute modulus bit length.
  • Check exponent sanity (odd, >1) and typical safe exponents (e.g. 65537).
  • Trial-divide modulus by small primes to detect trivially weak keys.
  • Recommendations (e.g. use ≥ 2048-bit modulus; use e=65537).
